        Where the facts come from

        The trophy list — names, descriptions, grades and hidden flags — rests on Exophase and PSNProfiles, as carried in the kit, cross-checked against the Output Lag and gtasix.how guides, which both cite GamesRadar+ and PC Gamer. It is not taken from PlayStation.com, which had not published the list when this guide was written. Each trophy's guide names its own sources in the detail panel, and every guide is written in our own words.
