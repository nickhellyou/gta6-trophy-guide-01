Missable trophies: one to watch

Only one trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude. Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 disappears once that chapter ends — follow her out to the balcony of Jason’s house and accept the optional task while you still can, and keep a Chapter 1 save until the trophy pops.

GTA VI has no mission replay and no New Game Plus. The game gives you 15 manual save slots plus one autosave: keep one save at the start of every chapter and one untouched save from just before the final mission, and rotate the rest. Never use cheats on your trophy save — they switch off trophies and saving.

Missable flag: PowerPyx trophy guide and GTABase, via the guides cited under that trophy.

Roadmap to the platinum

Built from the official descriptions: which chapters and missions, which collectibles and counts, in what order. Roughly 200 hours to the platinum at a difficulty of about 4/10 (PowerPyx) — long, not hard. No online trophies, no difficulty trophies.
